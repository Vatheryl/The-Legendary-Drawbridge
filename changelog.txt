1.4.1 - Haulox & Gargoyles
HAULOX ---
- Added Haulox, a large, harmless creature spawning in the Forest (especially near Hay or Spirit Reforged's Wheatgrass!)
	These come in 5 variations: Black, Brown, Ginger, Spotted, White
- Added Yokes, which can be used to lead Haulox
	There's a variation of these per Haulox variant as well
	Each is made with any wood @ a Sawmill, alongside some Cobwebs
- Added Ground Stakes, which can be used to hold Haulox
	Crafted @ a Sawmill with 8 of any wood

GARGOYLES ---
- Added 9 total Gargoyle statues:
Standard:
	- Crouching Gargoyle
	- Large Crouching Gargoyle
	- Large Lurking Gargoyle
	- Large Gargoyle
	- Poised Gargoyle
	- Crouching Gargoyle

Spouts: 
	- Feathered Gargoyle Spout
	- Lunging Gargoyle Spout
	- Gargoyle Spout

The Standard Gargoyles are just statues; the Spouts attach to walls and create a waterfall on their position.
All of these are created @ a Heavy Workbench with Stone (and a Water Bucket for Spouts).

BUGFIXES ---
- Delayed "functionality projectiles" (such as the Tower Clock's hands) from spawning (from load to right before the world is updated, and only when at least 1 player is joined)
- Fixed Drawbridges destroying tiles directly to their right when facing right
