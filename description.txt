--- The Castle Era ---
commissioner, lead: Vatheryl
programming, artwork: GabeHasWon
artwork: Gobiidaiah
artwork: Blade

The Castle Era (TCE) is a medieval-themed mod that adds in decorations, mechanisms and other tiles from the Dark Ages.
From Drawbridges to Portcullises, to Horse, Haulox and both of their armor, alongside a *ton* more stuff, TCE adds in a TON to play with.

Recipes for some notable tiles, but NOT all:

Drawbridges: 50 Wood, 30 Gray Bricks, 15 Iron/Lead Bars, 10 Chains @ Heavy Work Bench
Portcullises: 10 Wood, 5 Gray Bricks @ Heavy Work Bench
Spiked Palisades: 20 Wood, 5 Iron/Lead Bars @ Work Bench
Tower Clock: 30 Stone Block, 10 Glass, 5 Gold Bar, 3 Chain @ Heavy Work Bench
Stable Gates: 30 Wood, 2 Gold/Platinum Bars @ Work Bench
Horse Armors: 6-12 Ore Bars, 4 Silk @ Anvil
Magical Bookshelves: 20 Books, 30 Ebonwood/Shadewood, 8 Shadow Scales/Tissue Samples, 3 Fallen Stars @ Heavy Workbench
Machicolations: 1 Cauldron, Water Bucket, 1 Campfire, 20 Wood, 30 Stone Block @ Heavy Work Bench
Table Leg: 3 Wood @ Sawmill (makes 2)
Table Platform: 2 Wood @ Sawmill
Red Tablecloth: 2 Silk, Red Dye @ Loom

NPC Signs: Sold by respective town NPC
