--- Legendary Drawbridge & Castle Additions ---

Mod commissioned by Vatheryl

Drawbridges:
For castle moats and entrances...
- 2 Tiles wide - 15 Tiles tall
- Regular Drawbridge Recipe: 50 Wood + 30 Gray Bricks + 15 Iron/Lead Bars + 10 Chains at Heavy Work Bench
- Hell Drawbridge Recipe: 30 Obsidian + 15 Hellstone Bricks + 10 Chains at Heavy Work Bench
- Lion Drawbridge Recipe: 30 Gold/Platinum Bricks + 20 Marble + 10 Chains at Heavy Work Bench

Portcullises:
Classic castle gate barriers...
- 1 Tile wide - 8 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 10 Wood + 5 Gray Bricks at Heavy Work Bench

Spiked Palisades:
Defensive wooden barriers with spikes, inflict Bleeding on contact
- 3 Tiles wide - 6 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 20 Wood + 5 Iron/Lead Bars at Work Bench

Tower Clock & Bells:
Large decorative clock and chimeable bells for castle towers
- Tower Clock: 5 Tiles wide - 5 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 30 Stone Block + 10 Glass + 5 Gold Bar + 3 Chain at Heavy Work Bench
- Golden Bell: 3 Tiles wide - 3 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 10 Gold/Platinum Bars + 2 Chain at Anvil
- Graveyard Bell: 3 Tiles wide - 3 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 10 Iron/Lead Bars + 2 Chain + 5 Bone at Anvil (in a Graveyard)

Stable Gates:
Store and display your mounts
- Stable Gate: 9 Tiles wide - 6 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 30 Wood + 2 Gold/Platinum Bars at Work Bench
- Holds 1 mount in Singleplayer (decorative in Multiplayer)

- Stable Gate Extended: 27 Tiles wide - 6 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 1 Stable Gate + 60 Wood + 2 Gold/Platinum Bars at Work Bench
- Holds up to 4 mounts in Singleplayer (decorative in Multiplayer)

Horse Armors:
- Visual and defensive upgrades for horse mounts
- Equine Armature: Display mannequin for horse armor - Recipe: 40 Wood at Sawmill
- Copper Horse Armor: 6 Copper/Tin Bars + 4 Silk at Anvil
- Silver Horse Armor: 6 Silver/Tungsten Bars + 4 Silk at Anvil
- Iron Field Barding: 8 Iron/Lead Bars + 5 Silk at Anvil
- Golden Regal Barding: 10 Gold/Platinum Bars + 8 Silk + 1 Iron Field Barding at anvil
- Inferno Barding: 12 Hellstone Bars + 5 Obsidian + 1 Gold Armor at Hellforge

Equip in the new slot next to the Mount one
While mounted with armor equipped: +3 Defense (Iron), +5 Defense (Gold), +8 Defense (Molten)

Magical Bookshelves:
Dark teleportation portals disguised as ancient bookcases
- Doorcase: 3 Tiles wide - 4 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 20 Books + 30 Ebonwood/Shadewood + 8 Shadow Scales/Tissue Samples + 3 Fallen Stars at Heavy Workbench
- Right-click to open/close - step into open doorcases to teleport between paired ones
- Automatically closes after teleportation with dark dust effects

NPC Signs:
Professional signage for your town
- 19 different signs for each NPC profession
- Sign posts for mounting signs
- Purchased from its respective town-npc

Machicolations:
Medieval siege defense - pour boiling water on invaders!
- 10 Tiles wide - 6 Tiles tall
- Recipe: 1 Cauldron + 1 Water Bucket + 1 Campfire + 20 Wood + 30 Stone Block at Heavy Work Bench
- Right-click to activate - boils water and pours it down below
- Boiling water damages enemies and players for 3 seconds before evaporating
- 3 minute cooldown between uses
---
- Damages players / enemies on crushing
- Can be activated with wires
- Bells can be wire-connected to a Tower Clock to automatically chime at morning/evening
- Bells also give buffs to nearby players when chimed
- Horses will occasionally make noises and drop poop blocks every morning/evening

Black Culvert wall type added (uses vanilla Wall_94 texture)
